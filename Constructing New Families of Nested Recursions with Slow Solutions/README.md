# Constructing New Families of Nested Recursions with Slow Solutions

A research paper presenting a general method for generating families of nested recursions with slow
(sub-linear) solutions, starting from any two-term generalized Conolly or generalized Conway recursion
whose initial recursion already has a known slow solution. The method is applied to derive new
recursion families based on the Hofstadter $V$ and Conway recursions.
