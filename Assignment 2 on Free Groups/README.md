# Group Theory - A2 (2017)

My second assignment for my Group theory course. Covers free products and free groups (e.g. proving
$A*B$ is free iff $A$ and $B$ are free, via Nielsen-Schreier) and actions on Cayley graphs (Chapter 3),
and properties of the Baumslag-Solitar group $BS(1,2)$ and the group $S^G$ of dyadic translations
(Chapter 4).
